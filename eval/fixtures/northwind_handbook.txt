NORTHWIND LOGISTICS - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (2026 edition)

1. Working hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Employees may schedule the remaining hours flexibly. Remote work is allowed up to three days per week; the office anchor days are Tuesday and Thursday.

2. Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year, and carried-over days expire on 31 March. Leave requests longer than 10 consecutive days must be approved by a director at least 30 days in advance.

3. Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required once an absence exceeds 3 consecutive days. Sick days do not carry over.

4. Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

5. Expenses and travel
Business travel must be booked through the Northwind travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. The daily meal allowance while travelling is 55 USD. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days, and any single receipt above 250 USD needs manager approval before purchase.

6. Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 USD per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel is charged to the travel budget, not the learning budget.

7. Equipment
New hires receive a laptop within 5 business days of their start date. Laptops are replaced every 4 years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.

8. Security
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company systems. Customer data may never be copied to personal devices. Suspected phishing emails should be forwarded to security@northwind.example.

9. Notice period
The standard notice period is 60 days for staff and 90 days for managers and above. During probation (the first 3 months) either side may terminate with 14 days of notice.

10. Performance reviews
Reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Ratings use a four-point scale: Below, Meets, Exceeds, Outstanding. Salary changes are decided only after the December review.
